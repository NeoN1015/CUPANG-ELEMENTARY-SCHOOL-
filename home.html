Serving Barangay Cupang since 1932

Cupang Elementary School is a public elementary school in Barangay Cupang, Muntinlupa City, under the Department of Education through the Schools Division Office of Muntinlupa City.

It continues to provide quality education to the residents of Barangay Cupang and its neighboring areas.
