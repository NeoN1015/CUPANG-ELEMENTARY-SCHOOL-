# Surface brief: CES public site (home, about, achievements, contact)

Mode: Persuade (home), Read (about, achievements), Operate (contact form, staff dashboards inherit the world in restrained form).
Audience: community members and DepEd visitors first; parents and pupils second.
Job: learn who the school is, see what's coming on the calendar, reach the principal.
Proof on hand: the seal, the real facade photo (cesbg), facility photos, CESMAIN posters, city poster-making results, Women's Month poster. Sample content (orgs, extra achievements) stays visibly labelled SAMPLE.
Constraints: English; keep seal, school green, motto, DepEd core values; no invented stats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the schoolhouse front: a hand-painted green signboard over a row of working window bays. It refuses the photo-hero-plus-card-grid school template.

OWN-WORLD: Enamel board green (#1f5a32) owns the signboard, footer and section boards; deep green (#0f3a20) is the shadowed wall; narra wood (#7a4a2a) frames bays as thick mullions; capiz pane (#e4ebe3) is the reading surface inside bays; torch gold (#e9b949) from the seal's flame is the one accent and the today marker. Signage caps in Big Shoulders Display; body in Atkinson Hyperlegible. No textures, no faux distress.

STORY: Visitors recognise the school by its seal and lettered name, see that it is real and active (next events, latest posters), understand its 1932 roots, and write to the principal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wood eave and nav strip; full-width green signboard with the seal left and "Cupang Elementary School" painted at display scale, a gold line "EST. 1932 · SCHOOL ID 136912 · MUNTINLUPA CITY". Below it a three-bay window: live next events with today marker and "Open the full calendar" (primary action), the latest CESMAIN poster, and Visit/Write to the principal.
